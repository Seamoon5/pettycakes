Our story

A small kitchen with one simple idea

PettyCakes started with one oven, one fridge and a lot of birthdays. Seven years later the oven is bigger, but the way we work has not changed at all.

Nothing is made the day before. When your order comes in, we bake the sponge that morning, fill it, frost it and box it — so what you collect is still soft enough to eat with a fork. That is the whole reason people keep coming back instead of buying a cake from a shelf.

We also do the small things properly: a proper crumb, a filling that is not too sweet, and writing that is neat enough to photograph. If you have an idea in your head, describe it in the order form and we will tell you honestly what is possible.
