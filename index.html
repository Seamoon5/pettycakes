About PettyCakes

A small kitchen built for one thing:
cakes worth celebrating

PettyCakes is an online cake bakery. You pick a cake and tell us how you want it, our team gets the notification, and we start baking. There is no cart to fight with and no checkout that loses your message — you talk to the people who make the cake.

01

You choose a cake

Five signature flavours, each one priced clearly. Every card tells you the flavour, the filling and what it is usually decorated with.

02

You send one short form

Your name, contact number, the date you need it and any customisation — the writing on top, the colours, extra filling. That is all we need to start.

03

We bake it fresh for you

Our team gets your order notification, confirms the details with you on WhatsApp, then bakes, fills, frosts and packs it — ready for pickup or delivery.

Good to know: we bake to order, so please give us at least 48 hours notice. Allergens, egg-free, less sugar or a different size — put it in the customisation box and we will tell you honestly what is possible.
