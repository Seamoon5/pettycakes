# Image credits & licences

The photos in this project are **free placeholder stock**, chosen so the site looks
real while you decide on your own product photography. Every one of them was opened
and checked to make sure it actually shows the cake it is labelled with.

**Replace them with your own cake photos before you launch.** Keep the file names and
nothing else needs to change.

### Note on the two TheMealDB images

`cake-chocolate-truffle.jpg` and `cake-pistachio.jpg` come from
[TheMealDB](https://www.themealdb.com) free API. TheMealDB does not publish a
per-image Creative Commons licence, and their terms are written for their site rather
than for re-use elsewhere. They are fine as a temporary placeholder, but **do not rely
on them for a live shop** — swap in your own photos (or properly licensed stock) before
you take real orders.

### CC BY / CC BY-SA obligations

If you keep any of the Flickr images, the licence requires you to credit the
photographer. The simplest way to comply is to keep this file in the project and add a
small "Photo credits" line to your footer. If you would rather not deal with it, replace
the images — that obligation disappears with them.

## Icons in `assets/`

`favicon-32.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192.png` and `icon-512.png` are drawn
from scratch by `tools/make_icons.py` (pure Python standard library, no external
libraries). They are yours to use, modify and ship — no attribution needed.

The header logo in `index.html` is an inline SVG using the same cake-slice design.
